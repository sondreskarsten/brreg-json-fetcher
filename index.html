BRREG annual accounts

Unofficial mirror maintained by sondreskarsten. Not operated, approved or endorsed by Brønnøysundregistrene. Inneholder data under NLOD 2.0 tilgjengeliggjort av Brønnøysundregistrene.

Download the monthly dataset

Every month all Norwegian AS and ASA entities with a filed annual account are fetched once and published as a data-YYYY-MM release. Use the finished dataset instead of calling the API for each company.

Looking up the latest data release…

All releases, including recovery checkpoints: github.com/sondreskarsten/brreg-json-fetcher/releases. Verify SHA256SUMS after downloading.

Look up one organisation

Fetches https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/{orgnr} directly from your browser and returns the JSON as delivered by Brønnøysundregistrene. Nothing passes through this site.



The default response holds up to three fiscal years per type, KONSERN before SELSKAP. 404 means no accounts are available through this endpoint. A 500 with an unsupported-plan message covers plans such as BANK, IDEELL and PENSJ.
